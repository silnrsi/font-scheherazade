Scheherazade New - Honorific ligatures

Unicode has a number of honorific ligatures. The below chart should aid in understanding in what context they should be used. As new characters, applications may have problems with rendering them in right-to-left text. In some cases, you can correct reading order problems by inserting U+200F RIGHT-TO-LEFT MARK after the honorific ligature.

To view the simplified alternate glyphs and for instructions in accessing the simplified alternate glyphs, see the Features document.

SUBHAANAHU WA TAAALAA FDFE ﷾ May He be praised and exalted (Glorified and Lofty).
(The most common honorific for God.)
AZZA WA JALL FDFF ﷿ The Glorified/Exalted/Mighty and Sublime (Prestigious and Majestic).
(The second most common honorific for God.)
JALLA WA-ALAA FBC3 ﯃ Majestic and Exalted is He.
(Used after the name of God.)
JALLAJALALOUHOU FDFB ﷻ May His glory be glorified.
(Used after the name of God.)
TABAARAKA WA-TAAALAA FD4E ﵎ May he be blessed and exalted (Blessed and Lofty).
(One of the honorifics used only for God himself.)
Unicode Name
ARABIC LIGATURE... (for prophets)		 USV Glyph Meaning
ALAYHI AS-SALAAM FD47 ﵇ Peace be upon him.
(The normal honorific used after a prophet's or an Archangel's name.)
ALAYHIM AS-SALAAM FD48 ﵈ Peace be upon them (masculine plural).
(Used for two or more prophets.)
ALAYHIMAA AS-SALAAM FD49 ﵉ Peace be upon them (dual).
(Used for prophets and angels.)
ALAYHAA AS-SALAAM FD4D ﵍ Peace be upon her.
(Used after the name of a woman who was the mother of a prophet.)
ALAYHI AS-SALAATU WAS-SALAAM FD4A ﵊ Blessings and Peace be upon him.
(A lesser used honorific used after a prophet's or an Archangel's name.)
ALAYHAA AS-SALAATU WAS-SALAAM 10ED1 𐻑 Blessings and Peace be upon her.
(Used almost exclusively for respected female figures such as Mary, the mother of Jesus.)
ALAYHIM AS-SALAATU WAS-SALAAM 10ED2 𐻒 Blessings and Peace be upon them (plural).
(Used when mentioning multiple prophets, messengers, or angels.)
ALAYHIMAA AS-SALAATU WAS-SALAAM 10ED3 𐻓 Blessings and Peace be upon them (dual).
(Used when mentioning two revered figures together.)

RADI ALLAAHU ANH FD41 ﵁ May God be pleased with him.
(Used for companions of the prophet.)
RADI ALLAAHU ANHAA FD42 ﵂ May God be pleased with her.

ALAYHI AR-RAHMAH FBD2 ﯒ Mercy be upon him.
(Used after mentioning the name of a religious person or teacher who has passed away.)
RAHIMAHU ALLAAH FD40 ﵀ May God have mercy upon him.
(Used for companions of the prophet or other widely recognized scholars. Can be for any believer who has passed away.)
RAHIMAHUM ALLAAH FD4F ﵏ May God have mercy upon them (masculine).
(Used for widely recognized scholars. Also used for ordinary believers who have passed away.)
RAHIMAHUMAA ALLAAH FBC9 ﯉ May God have mercy upon both of them.
(Used for two people who have passed away.)
RAHMATU ALLAAHI ALAYHIM FBC6 ﯆ May God have mercy upon them (plural).
(Used for relatives or students of companions of the prophet who have passed away.)
RAHMATU ALLAAHI ALAYHIMAA FBC7 ﯇ May God have mercy upon them (masculine and feminine plural).
(Used for relatives or students of companions of the prophet who have passed away.)
RAHMATU ALLAAHI ALAYH FD90 ﶐ May God have mercy upon him (masculine singular).
(Used for relative or student of companions of the prophet who has passed away.)
RAHMATU ALLAAHI ALAYHAA FD91 ﶑ May God have mercy upon her (feminine singular).
(Used for relative or student of companions of the prophet who has passed away.)

BISMILLAH AR-RAHMAN AR-RAHEEM FDFD ﷽ In the name of God, the most merciful, the most compassionate.
(Used as the opening of each action in order to receive blessing from God.)
SALLALLAAHU ALAYHI WA-AALIH FD46 ﵆ Blessings of God be upon him and the people of his household.
(Used particularly in Shia Islam for leaders who come from the bloodline of the prophet.)
SALLALLAHU ALAYHI WA-AALIHEE WA-SALLAM FD4C ﵌ Blessings of God and peace be upon him and the people of his household.
(Used by all Muslims, but particularly in Shia Islam for the prophet Muhammad.)
SALLALLAHOU ALAYHE WASALLAM FDFA ﷺ Blessings of God and peace be upon him.
(Used after the name of a major prophet, especially the prophet of Islam.)
SALAAMUHU ALAYNAA FDCF ﷏ Blessings of God and peace be upon us.
(Used by some followers of Jesus to give special honor to him.)
QADDASA ALLAAHU SIRRAH 10ED4 𐻔 May his innermost being be sanctified.
(Used especially within Sufism when mentioning a revered saint or spiritual teacher.)
QUDDISA SIRRAH FD4B ﵋ May his innermost being be sanctified.
(Used for Sufi saints, and refers to their shrines where people come to worship.)
QUDDISA SIRRUHUM 10ED5 𐻕 May their innermost being be purified (plural).
(Used especially within Sufism.)
QUDDISA SIRRUHUMAA 10ED6 𐻖 May their innermost being be purified (dual).
(Used especially within Sufism for two people.)
QUDDISAT ASRAARUHUM 10ED7 𐻗 May their innermost being be purified (plural).
(Used especially within Sufism for a group of people.)
DAAMAT BARAKAATUHUM FBC4 ﯄ May their blessings continue (feminine plural).
(Used for living scholars.)
HAFIZAHU ALLAAH FBCC ﯌ May God protect/preserve him (masculine singlular).
(Used when mentioning a living scholar, teacher, or leader.)
HAFIZAHUM ALLAAHU TAAALAA FBCE ﯎ May God protect/preserve them (plural).
(Used when mentioning three or more living people.)
HAFIZAHUMAA ALLAAHU TAAALAA FBCF ﯏ May God protect/preserve them (dual, masculine or feminine).
(Used when mentioning two living people.)
AJJAL ALLAAHU FARAJAHU ASH-SHAREEF FBD1 ﯑ May God hasten his noble reappearance.
(Used particularly in Shia Islam for the 12th Imam.)
KARRAMA ALLAAHU WAJHAH FDCE ﷎ May God honor his face.
(Used almost exclusively for the cousin and son-in-law of the prophet of Islam.)
NAWWARA ALLAAHU MARQADAH 10ED8 𐻘 May God illuminate his resting place.
(Used in reference to someone who has passed away.)
